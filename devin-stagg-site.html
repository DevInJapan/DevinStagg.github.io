Coaching with me

Through Kobe Eigomura, the practice I run with my wife, I work with English learners at three levels — pick the one that matches where you are.

  • Group conversation Weekly Friday-evening sessions — the entry point, open to anyone building conversational confidence.
  • Small-group coaching Cohorts built around a shared goal, such as business English or exam preparation.
  • One-on-one coaching Premium coaching tied to a specific outcome — a job interview, a certification, a career move.
